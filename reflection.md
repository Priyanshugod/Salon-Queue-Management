# Reflection — AI-Assisted Development

During the development of the Salon Queue Management System, I used an AI coding agent to assist with documentation, implementation, debugging, and deployment preparation.

I believe that providing clear, detailed, and structured prompts played an important role in the development process. I specified the required screens, functionalities, design preferences, queue management rules, and implementation steps before beginning development.

I did not encounter any major mistakes from the AI agent that affected the overall implementation. The generated work largely followed the requirements I provided. This demonstrated how effective well-structured prompts can be when working with AI coding tools.

One important observation from the process was that AI-generated code and documentation should not automatically be considered production-ready. Even when the output appears correct, it is important to review the implementation, test the application's functionality, verify real-time synchronization, and check deployment compatibility.

Whenever I needed additional clarification, I refined my instructions and asked the agent to review specific parts of the project.

Overall, this project helped me understand the importance of prompt engineering, iterative development, and human verification when building applications with AI assistance.
